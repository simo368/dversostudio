# DVERSO STUDIO — Design system

## Direction
Luxury editorial studio, cinematic photography, high-contrast serif typography, black space, champagne details. Avoid generic SaaS patterns, pill buttons, rounded cards, icon grids and decorative gradients.

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond, restrained italic contrast.
- Utility/body: Manrope.
- Keep copy narrow, labels small and tracked, titles large with tight leading and tracking.

## Motion
- Lead with the image reveal, then the italic kicker, then title lines.
- Use GSAP timelines for choreography and ScrollTrigger for entrance/parallax.
- Use Lenis only when reduced motion is not requested.
- Animate transform and opacity; avoid layout animation.
- Motion must have a reason and stay subtle.

## Layout
- Generous negative space and thin champagne separators.
- Asymmetric portfolio grid, sharp image edges, typography-led services.
- Mobile layouts simplify composition rather than shrinking desktop layouts.
